# WhatsApp setup status

**There is no WhatsApp setup step for crypto-futures signal delivery in this application.** Meta's current WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy restricts promoting or facilitating the exchange of virtual currency. The app therefore does not collect a WhatsApp phone-number ID, access token or verify token and does not contain an outbound crypto-signal sender.

Use the authenticated web dashboard at the application's own URL to receive and review paper-research signals. Do not attempt to enable crypto-signal delivery through unofficial WhatsApp Web automation, alternate business identities or disguised message wording.

Reassess only after an official Meta policy change and explicit written confirmation that this exact service and message purpose is permitted. A general WhatsApp Business account or template approval is not sufficient to override the restricted-business policy.
