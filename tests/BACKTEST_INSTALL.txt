V11 FIXED BACKTEST

The paper backtester uses only completed 1D / 12H / 4H / 1H candles.
12H is synthesized causally from three contiguous completed 4H candles.
Each decision is made at a completed 1H close and paper execution uses the next 1H open.

Supported windows:
BACKTEST 1D
BACKTEST 7D
BACKTEST 30D
BACKTEST 60D
BACKTEST 90D
BACKTEST 180D
BACKTEST 365D

For strategy evaluation, compare LONG/SHORT balance, TP/SL outcomes, total R,
expectancy, profit factor, drawdown, signal RR, and actual-fill RR across multiple windows.

This is a paper simulator. No real orders are executed by the supplied release.
